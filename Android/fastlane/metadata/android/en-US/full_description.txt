FireDrop moves files and whole folders directly between devices on the same local Wi-Fi network - phone to computer, computer to phone, and phone to phone. No internet, no cloud, no accounts: the transfer does not leave the room.

Features:
- Folders are sent whole; nested folder structure is preserved.
- Speed is limited only by the Wi-Fi network (tens of MB/s is realistic).
- Connection by scanning a QR code or by a six-digit code with limited attempts.
- Incoming files are never overwritten: duplicates get number-suffixed names (((1), (2)...)).
- Works in the foreground service when receiving, with a persistent status notification.

Traffic is plain HTTP inside the local network only. The app serves and connects to explicit IP:port addresses shown on the screen; no data is sent elsewhere.
